◆ Our Story

From two founders to a full engineering studio

OovaTech began in 2026 with a simple frustration: too much technology was overcomplicated, oversold, and under-delivered. We set out to do the opposite — ship real, working software that solves real problems.

A decade later, we've grown into a team of engineers, designers, and strategists serving clients worldwide — while keeping the same craft-first mindset we started with.
